Approval flow

A quote goes through the steps that apply to it, in order, before it can be sent. A step applies when any of its conditions holds. Only members with the step's role (or administrators) approve it; with no role, anyone allowed to change quotes. Rejecting needs a reason and sends the quote back to its preparer.
