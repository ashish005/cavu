A rule reacts when something happens to a record: it starts a workflow (e.g. a vendor member is created → Vendor onboarding) or sends a notification by email, in-app, SMS or push to people chosen by rules, with attachments. Processes are linked to their record, so their stages can also switch on that record's later events. Your own record types work too: send their events to POST /api/v1/process/events.
